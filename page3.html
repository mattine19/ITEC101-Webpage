Our Story

Lexington Medical Center has been serving the Midlands of South Carolina since 1971. What began as a community healthcare organization has grown into a comprehensive healthcare network dedicated to improving the health of the people it serves.

Our hospital provides a wide range of medical services, advanced technology, and specialized treatments. We believe every patient deserves compassionate care, respect, and attention throughout their healthcare journey.

Our mission is to provide quality healthcare close to home while continuing to support the communities and families throughout the Midlands.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Lexington Medical Center located?

Our main campus is located at 2720 Sunset Boulevard in West Columbia, South Carolina.

How can I schedule an appointment?

You can contact Lexington Medical Center or use the online resources available through its official website.

Can I access my medical records online?

Yes! Patients can use LMC MyChart to access health information and manage aspects of their healthcare.

Does the hospital have emergency services?

Yes. The Emergency Department provides emergency medical services 24 hours a day.
