Our Medical Services

Lexington Medical Center provides comprehensive healthcare services to individuals and families throughout the Midlands of South Carolina. Our goal is to provide high-quality care, advanced technology, and compassionate service to every patient.

Explore Our Services

Emergency Care

Emergency medical care for serious illnesses, injuries, and unexpected medical situations. Our emergency department is available around the clock.

Heart & Vascular Care

Specialized services focused on heart health, cardiovascular conditions, prevention, and treatment.

Women's & Maternity Care

Healthcare services for women, including pregnancy support, maternity services, and specialized medical care.

Surgical Services

Surgical procedures supported by experienced medical professionals and advanced medical technology.

Imaging & Radiology

Diagnostic imaging services that help healthcare professionals identify, evaluate, and monitor medical conditions.

Primary Care

Routine checkups, preventive care, health screenings, and ongoing support for your overall health.
